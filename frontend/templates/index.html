The pipeline

Seven modules, one continuous line from data to decision

Each step feeds the next — your profile becomes a simulation, the simulation becomes a risk score, and the score becomes a plan you can actually follow.

01

Digital Health Profile

Age, vitals, lifestyle and family history are captured once and stored securely — the foundation every other module reads from.

MongoDB · health_profiles
02

Future Health Simulation

A transparent, rule-based model projects your BMI, blood pressure, glucose and cholesterol forward year by year under your current habits.

Pandas · NumPy
03

Disease Risk Prediction

A Random Forest classifier trained on weighted clinical risk factors scores your Low / Moderate / High risk with a confidence level.

Scikit-learn · Random Forest
04

Explainable AI

Every prediction ships with the ranked factors that drove it — no black box, just the reasoning behind your number.

Feature importance ranking
05

AI Health Coach

Your dashboard translates risk factors into a prioritized, plain-language action list — updated every time you re-check your risk.

Flask API
06

Preventive Recommendations

Concrete, categorized next steps — nutrition, screening, sleep, stress — ranked by how much they move your risk.

MongoDB · recommendations
07

Interactive Health Timeline

Past check-ins and future projections sit on one horizon line, so you can see exactly where today's choices lead.

Chart.js
